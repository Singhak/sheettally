How It Works in 3 Steps

From customer drawing to billable PDF proposal in under 10 seconds.

01

📁 1. Drop DXF File

Drag & drop any AutoCAD ASCII 2D DXF or SVG. The engine heals broken polylines, connects open contours, detects inch vs mm units, and isolates outer borders from internal cutouts.

02

⚙️ 2. Engine Computes

Instantly computes exact linear cut inches, total pierce points, machine cycle time based on 4kW–12kW feed tables, assist gas consumption, and packs parts onto 4×8 or 5×10 sheets.

03

📄 3. Get Commercial Quote

Download a branded Net 30 commercial PDF quote, copy an itemized email breakdown, or share directly to WhatsApp. Machine operator job card generated automatically.

Manufacturing Intelligence

Built Specifically for Sheet Metal Job Shops

Everything required to price jobs accurately without waiting on heavy desktop CAM seats.

📐

Auto-Sanitizing DXF & Polyline Healer

Customer CAD files are notoriously messy. SheetDXF heals fragmented vectors, bridges micro-gaps, identifies outer perimeters vs interior cutouts, and detects drawing units automatically.

⚡

Calibrated Fiber Laser Feed Rates

Not rough guesses. Speeds are based on calibrated cutting tables for 4kW, 6kW, 10kW, and 12kW fiber lasers across Carbon Steel (A36), Stainless (304/316), and Aluminum (5052/6061) with N2, O2, and Air.

🔲

True-Shape 2D Sheet Nesting

Packs single parts or multi-part assembly batches onto standard 4×8 ft, 5×10 ft, or custom mill sheets. Accounts for kerf spacing, sheet margins, and calculates true skeleton scrap percentage.

🔍

Automated DFM Hole Blowout Check

Enforces the 1:1 thickness rule. Flags hole diameters smaller than plate thickness (d < t) where thermal blowout risks laser nozzle damage, with a 1-click auto-skip toggle.

📄

Net 30 Proforma Quotes & Job Cards

One-click professional manufacturing PDF proposals itemizing cutting labor, piercing, plate handling, and secondary operations (bending, tapping, powder coat) plus machine operator cut sheets.
